About the Project

"O Allah, make me recognize Your Proof, for if You do not make me recognize Your Proof, I will deviate from my religion."

— Rasool-e-Khuda (S.A.W.A.), Al-Kafi, Vol. 1, p. 337; Bihar al-Anwar, Vol. 52, p. 146

The Lesser Known Imams (as) [TLKI] is an initiative born out of a sense of duty toward the legacy of the Ahlul Bayt (as). It is a dedicated effort to ensure that the light of their guidance reaches every seeker of truth.

While the names of all our Aimmah (as) are held sacred, the specific wisdom, struggles, and detailed lives of some of our Imams (as) are often discussed less frequently in mainstream gatherings. TLKI is a devoted attempt to bridge this gap, focusing on the Ma'refat of those Imams (as) whose narrations and profound sacrifices are sometimes mentioned only in passing.

We hold the firm belief that the words of the Ahlul Bayt (as) are a source of illumination for the soul, as echoed in Ziyarat al-Jameah al-Kabeera:

كَلامُكُمْ نُورٌ، وَاَمْرُکُمْ رُشْدٌ

"Your speech is light, and your command is guidance."

No part of this light should remain hidden from the hearts of the believers.

Our Path of Service:

  • Spreading the Word: Curating and sharing verified Hadiths and historical accounts of the Ahlul Bayt (as).
  • Establishing Majalis: Organizing gatherings specifically dedicated to the Imams (as) in focus.
  • Building Community: Creating a space where believers can unite under the banner of knowledge and devotion to the Ahlul Bayt (as).

We are merely servants standing at their door, hoping that this small effort is accepted in their holy presence.
